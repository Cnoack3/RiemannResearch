# Approximating the single density image B g₂

Date: 2026-11-12 (America/Los_Angeles).

Module: [RH/QDensityParityApproximation.lean](RH/QDensityParityApproximation.lean).

## Result and scope

The requested membership
\[
B g_2\in V,\qquad V=\overline{\operatorname{span}}^Q\{g_k:k\ge2\},
\]
remains **Open**. The module constructs actual finite inverse-Gram fits,
identifies them as the orthogonal projections for every nonempty stage,
and proves their complete error identities. It does not prove that the
limiting error vanishes and does not assert RH.

The matrix is the complete Q Gram matrix, with all conductors 2 through
N+1. No truncation of the physical probability law is used.

## Explicit target and coefficients

Let \(z=\zeta(2)\). The operator B is specifically QDensityMetric.B=dQ/dP;
it is not the shifted reciprocal operator bearing the same letter in
QShiftedReciprocal.

\[
u(n):=Dg_2(n)=\frac{\mathbf1_{\{n\ {\rm odd}\}}}{2n},
\qquad Bg_2=z^{-1}(g_2+u).
\]
Because \(g_2\in V\),
\[
Bg_2\in V\iff u\in V.
\]
The right-hand side of the complete normal equations is
\[
(b_N^{(2)})_i
 =\langle g_{i+2},u\rangle_Q
 =\sum_{\substack{m\ge1\\m\ {\rm odd}}}
   \frac{m\bmod(i+2)}{2z(i+2)m^3}.
\]
Put \(c_N=G_N^{-1}b_N^{(2)}\), \(f_N=\sum_{i<N}(c_N)_i g_{i+2}\), and
\[
h_N=z^{-1}(g_2+f_N).
\]
Every \(h_N\) is a finite legal residue combination. For N>0 it is exactly
the Q-orthogonal projection of Bg₂ onto the stage generated by
g₂,...,g_{N+1}.

## Exact error and the open limit

Lean verifies
\[
\|Bg_2-h_N\|_Q^2
 =z^{-2}\left(\|u\|_Q^2-(b_N^{(2)})^\top G_N^{-1}b_N^{(2)}\right),
\]
and the exact complete target norm
\[
\|u\|_Q^2
 =\sum_{\substack{m\ge1\\m\ {\rm odd}}}\frac1{4z m^4}.
\]
The elementary evaluations of zeta(2), zeta(4), and the odd part of the
fourth-power sum give
\[
\|u\|_Q^2=\frac{15\zeta(4)}{64\zeta(2)}=\frac{\pi^2}{64}.
\]
This final scalar evaluation is a paper calculation here; the Lean
declaration retains the exact infinite series.

The proved unconditional error limit is
\[
\|Bg_2-h_N\|_Q^2\longrightarrow
 z^{-2}\|u-\Pi_Vu\|_Q^2.
\]
The required new assertion is
\[
(b_N^{(2)})^\top G_N^{-1}b_N^{(2)}\longrightarrow\|u\|_Q^2.
\]
The formal zero-error criterion is an equivalence with Bg₂ membership,
not a proof of membership. It is a conditional research reduction with
the zero-limit obligation still **Open**.

The Parseval theorem retains every divisor-wavelet coordinate:
\[
\|Bg_2-h_N\|_Q^2
 =z^{-2}\sum_{r\ge1}
 \frac{\left(\langle u,w_r\rangle_Q-
 \sum_{i<N}(c_N)_i\,\mathrm{qResidueDivisorCoefficient}(i+2,r)\right)^2}
 {J_2(r)}.
\]

## Concrete coordinate interpolation

The finite residue combination
\[
f_3^{\rm head}=g_2+\tfrac13g_3-\tfrac49g_4
\]
agrees with u at physical coordinates 1,2,3. Hence
\[
h_3^{\rm head}=z^{-1}\left(2g_2+\tfrac13g_3-\tfrac49g_4\right)
\]
agrees with Bg₂ at those coordinates. Both identities are kernel checked.
These are exact coordinate interpolants, distinct from the inverse-Gram
least-squares fits above. No norm-convergence claim follows from the three
coordinate equalities.
