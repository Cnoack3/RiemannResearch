## Projection and Möbius cutoff order

For the complete signed pairing C(M,N) of the projection residual at M with the canonical Möbius residual at N, each fixed row tends to zero, while every column tends to the same limiting energy E. ## RH and the actual categorical unit

For each finite support stage, vanishing of its unit boundary is equivalent to RH. RH is also equivalent to the centered boundary being an epimorphism, to the constant lying in the closed squarefree Pareto span, and to natural Nyman–Beurling approximation with freely chosen coefficients. ## Two moving Gram solves
