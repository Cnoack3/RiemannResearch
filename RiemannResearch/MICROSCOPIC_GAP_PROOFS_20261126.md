# Microscopic gaps at actual zeta zeros

This guide maps the 21 claims in the November 26 proof request to their Lean declarations. Heights are unfolded by $L=\log T/(2\pi)$, and pair counts are divided by $D=TL$. A strict positive gap excludes ties. Chronological ranks refer to the list of occurrences, counting each complex zero with its analytic multiplicity. The distinct-height statistic counts an ordinate once regardless of how many complex zeros or copies share it.

## Actual zeros and distinct heights

The new transfer theorem has one analytic hypothesis. For every $\epsilon>0$ there is a fixed $R\ge1$ for which the actual triangle second moment is eventually at most $R^2+\epsilon R$. Under this hypothesis the occurrence and unweighted distinct-height sine laws are equivalent.

For the forward direction, let $K_b$ be the translated count at unfolded width $b=Q/k$. A window of width $Q$ splits into $k$ cells. For the integer cell counts, $(K_b-1)_+^2\le K_b(K_b-1)$. On the event that the large window contains more than $2k$ occurrences, subdivision and Cauchy–Schwarz yield
$$
 \frac1T\int K_Q^2\mathbf1_{K_Q>2k}
 \le 4k^2\frac1T\int K_{Q/k}(K_{Q/k}-1).
$$
For each fixed $b>0$, PCC and the short-window upper estimate give the limit $2\int_0^b G(u)\,du$ on the right before multiplication by $4k^2$, where $G(u)=\int_0^u(1-\operatorname{sinc}^2(\pi x))\,dx$. The integrand defining $G$ is continuous and vanishes at zero, so $\int_0^bG(u)\,du=o(b^2)$. Taking $k$ large first and then $T$ large proves uniform integrability of the translated square. The finite vacancy and correction bounds now transfer the occurrence law to distinct heights. In the reverse direction the distinct sine law supplies the affine lower floor. A persistent positive multiplicity correction would then exceed the cofinal triangle upper bounds.
