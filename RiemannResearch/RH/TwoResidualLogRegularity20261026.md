# Two residuals: logarithmic regularity and the contradiction

26 October 2026.

The Q/Pareto argument has a checked logarithmic-tail extension and a checked
physical polylogarithmic implication. These are conditional RH criteria:
they do not supply the actual displacement envelope and do not prove RH.

## Setting and exact comparison

For n>=1, use P(n)=1/[n(n+1)], Q(n)=1/[zeta(2)n^2], and the same full
squarefree closed physical span V generated by g_d(n)=(n mod d)/d.
Write f_Q=Pi_Q 1, f_P=Pi_P 1, r=1-f_Q, r_P=1-f_P, and
d=f_Q-f_P=r_P-r. All Pareto functions are identified with the same physical
functions in Q coordinates. Let Bf(n)=f(n)/(n+1).

The existing checked declarations give

    u = B r_P - d belongs to V^(perp_Q),
    zeta(2) <1,u>_Q = zeta(2) <r,B r>_Q + ||d||_P^2.

The second identity retains both nonnegative terms. If r is nonzero,
faithfulness of B makes mu=<1,u>_Q strictly positive.

The exact tail equality, before any estimate, is

    ||tail_N u||_Q^2
      = ||tail_N(B r_P)||_Q^2 + ||tail_N d||_Q^2
        - 2 <tail_N(B r_P),tail_N d>_Q.

Equivalent norms do not identify the two projections. No equality of
projections, extra orthogonality, positivity of projection, or multiplier
invariance is assumed.

## Fresh Lean result: every fixed logarithmic scale

Set L_N=6+log N for N>=1. The new source file is
RH/QSquarefreeLogDisplacement.lean, namespace

For every integer k>=0,

    r != 0 implies [N/L_N^k] ||tail_N d||_Q^2 -> +infinity.

In particular, for each fixed k,

    r=0 iff exists C, for arbitrarily large integers N,
                    [N/L_N^k] ||tail_N d||_Q^2 <= C.

This is the declaration residual_zero_iff_frequent_log_displacement_tail.
The declaration riemannHypothesis_of_frequent_log_displacement_tail applies

The supporting squarefree_annihilator_log_tail is unconditional: every
squarefree annihilator with nonzero mean has divergent logarithmically
normalized complete Q tails.

Proof: choose a prime p with N<p<=2N, set M=p-1, and use the legal squarefree
exact head at M. The exact signed pairing is

    <defect_M,tail_N u>_Q = <1,u>_Q.

The established defect_sq_log_weight_tendsto_zero gives

    ||defect_M||_Q^2 / [M/L_M^k] -> 0.

Since N<=M<=2N, positivity and monotonicity of the logarithm give

    M/L_M^k <= 2N/L_N^k.

Cauchy-Schwarz therefore forces the claimed tail escape. This step explicitly

## Polylogarithmic corollary: Lean verification and paper derivation

The implication in this section is now formalized. The modules
Analysis/PolylogTailProfile.lean and RH/QPolylogTailEnergy.lean prove, for
every QHilbert vector v and natural m, the complete tail estimate

    ||tail_N v||_Q^2 <= [2 C^2/zeta(2)] (6+log N)^(2m)/N,

assuming N>=1, 6+log N>=4m, and |v(n)|<=C(6+log n)^m for every n>N.
The exact full-series equality is tail_energy_physical; no finite tail is
substituted. eventually_polylog_scaled_tail_le handles eventual envelopes.

QSquarefreeLogDisplacement.residual_zero_iff_eventually_polylog_displacement
proves the equivalence with residual zero for each fixed natural exponent.
riemannHypothesis_of_eventually_real_polylog_displacement covers every fixed
real exponent by rounding it upward to a natural exponent. Its envelope
hypothesis is still OPEN. All new proof bodies checked with only propext,
Classical.choice, and Quot.sound; there are no additional domain axioms.

The following real-exponent quantitative derivation remains the paper
presentation. The exact displayed tail constant has been formalized for
natural exponents; the arbitrary-real-exponent RH implication is formalized
via rounding, rather than asserting that this whole derivation is Lean code.

Suppose eventually |d(n)|<=D L_n^beta, where D,beta>=0 are fixed.
The actual Pareto residual satisfies ||r_P||_P<=1, hence

    |B r_P(n)| <= sqrt[n/(n+1)] ||r_P||_P <= 1.

Thus |u(n)|<=(1+D)L_n^beta on a sufficiently large tail. Put A=1+D.
For L_N>=4 beta and n>N,

    (L_n/L_N)^(2 beta)
       <= exp[(2 beta/L_N) log(n/N)] <= (n/N)^(1/2).

The first inequality is log(1+x)<=x applied to
L_n/L_N=1+log(n/N)/L_N. Consequently, keeping the entire infinite tail,

    ||tail_N u||_Q^2
       <= A^2 L_N^(2 beta)/(zeta(2) sqrt N) * sum_{n>N} n^(-3/2)
       <= [2 A^2/zeta(2)] L_N^(2 beta)/N.

The last inequality is the integral comparison
sum_{n>N} n^(-3/2)<=2/sqrt N.
Choose an integer k>=2 beta. The normalized tail is bounded, contradicting
the freshly checked annihilator tail theorem if r!=0. Therefore

    some fixed polylogarithmic bound for d
       iff d=0 iff r=0 iff 1 belongs to V.

Only the implication from target membership to RH is used from Lean.
The actual polylogarithmic envelope has NOT been proved.

## Stronger quantitative paper extension

This section is also an ordinary proof. Let z=zeta(2) and
Phi(s)=(s log s)^(1/11). The published
QMobiusStretchedEnergy.exactHead_error_eventual_stretched_energy supplies
a>0 and C>=0 with

    ||defect_M||_Q^2 <= 16/z + C M exp[-a Phi(6+log M)]

eventually. At the prime cutoff above, monotonicity of Phi gives

    M exp[-a Phi(L_M)] <= 2N exp[-a Phi(L_N)].

Thus, eventually and uniformly for squarefree annihilators u,

    <1,u>_Q^2
       <= [16/z + 2C N exp(-a Phi(L_N))] ||tail_N u||_Q^2.

No exponent is lost; only C doubles. Put t_N=N exp[-a Phi(L_N)].
Then

    r=0 iff liminf_N t_N ||tail_N d||_Q^2 = 0.

Interpret liminf in the extended nonnegative reals, or equivalently require
an unbounded subsequence on which the displayed quantity tends to zero.

Proof: the bounded B r_P has t_N||tail_N(B r_P)||^2->0. The exact signed
tail identity and Cauchy-Schwarz imply the same subsequential limit for u
whenever it holds for d. Ordinary L2 tail decay removes the constant 16/z
term in the quantitative inequality. It follows that <1,u>=0, and the
positive comparison identity forces r=0. The converse follows from d=0.

For a concrete sufficient envelope, any

    |d(n)| <= D exp[b Phi(L_n)], with 0<=b<a/2,

eventually implies r=0. Indeed Phi is increasing and concave for s>1, with

    Phi'(s) = (log s+1)/[11(s log s)^(10/11)],
    Phi''(s) = -[10(log s)^2+9 log s+10]/[121(s log s)^(21/11)].

Its derivative tends to zero. For sufficiently large N,
2b Phi'(L_N)<=1/2, so concavity gives
exp[2b(Phi(L_n)-Phi(L_N))]<=(n/N)^(1/2). The same integral comparison gives

    ||tail_N u||^2 <= [2(1+D)^2/(zN)] exp[2b Phi(L_N)].

The quantitative pairing bound is then at most

    [32(1+D)^2/z^2] exp[2b Phi(L_N)]/N
      + [4C(1+D)^2/z] exp[-(a-2b)Phi(L_N)],

which tends to zero. This includes any envelope exp(o(Phi(L_n))).
At the threshold b=a/2, little-o suffices by an epsilon-tail argument;
a mere big-O bound is not supplied by this argument.

the two symbolic remainders were zero. No numerical RH inference is made.

## Remaining arithmetic obligation

A finite Hilbert norm, smoothing by B, or a contraction estimate for a
compressed operator does not establish physical polylogarithmic regularity.

With Pi=Pi_Q and A=Pi B restricted to V, the exact equation is

    d = Pi B r + A d.

The Neumann resolvent controls the Hilbert norm, but using it in a physical
weighted supremum norm requires a separate stability theorem. The task
remaining is to bound the actual signed response for this particular residual,
or to prove one of the weaker complete-tail conditions above.

## Actual displacement: signed kernel localization (paper result)

This subsection is an independently checked paper derivation, not a Lean
declaration. It applies to the actual full squarefree span and to each
matched finite span.

Let e_n^P(m)=1_{m=n}/P(n) and K_n^P=Pi_V^P e_n^P. Then
< v,K_n^P >_P=v(n) for v in V. Set Df(m)=f(m)/m.
Here r always means the Q residual r_Q, even in a P norm.
Since Q/P=(1+D)/zeta(2), the normal equations give exactly

    <d,v>_P = <r,Dv>_P,
    <r,(I+D)v>_P = 0       (v in V).

Subtracting 1/(n+1) times the second equation from the first, with
v=K_n^P, retains every signed term:

    d(n) = sum_{m>=1} P(m) r(m) [(n-m)/(m(n+1))] K_n^P(m).

The diagonal coefficient is zero. All sums converge absolutely by weighted
Cauchy--Schwarz; the multiplier has absolute value at most one.

Define the signed lower response

    L(n) = sum_{1<=m<n} P(m) r(m) [(n-m)/(m(n+1))] K_n^P(m).

On m>=n the multiplier is at most 1/(n+1) in absolute value.
Projection contractivity gives ||K_n^P||_P^2<=1/P(n)=n(n+1).
Consequently the complete remainder satisfies

    |d(n)-L(n)|^2
      <= [n/(n+1)] sum_{m>=n} P(m) r_Q(m)^2
      <= zeta(2) sum_{m>=n} Q(m) r_Q(m)^2 -> 0.

Thus d(n)=L(n)+o(1). This does not bound L(n).
For finite stages the same formula uses r_{Q,N}, K_{N,n}^P and d_N.
Convergence r_{Q,N}->r_Q gives uniform tail tightness, hence the remainder
is o(1) uniformly in N.

OPEN arithmetic obligation:

    |sum_{m<n} P(m) r_{Q,N}(m)
          [(n-m)/(m(n+1))] K_{N,n}^P(m)|
      <= C (6+log n)^a,

with common C,a over the required growing stages and heads. Neither generic
Hilbert estimates nor the CRT carry identities prove this signed bound.

## Generalized CRT and primary-source context

The generalized CRT suggestion is compatible with the existing legal carry
formalization: no pairwise-coprimality assumption is needed when an explicit
modular coefficient certificate is provided. For a=gA,b=gB and gcd(A,B)=1,
Bezout coefficients xB+yA=1 give the signed carry at L=gAB. Once the old
span contains g_a and g_b, its screened carry is exactly -screen(g_L).
This changes coordinates without supplying an inverse-Gram estimate.

The primary article by Baez-Duarte, Berrizbeitia and Iribarren (2002),
[Sobre el teorema chino del resto](https://acfiman.org/boletines_articulos/sobre-el-teorema-chino-del-resto/),
provides congruence compatibility and an abstract-ring CRT. It supplies no
projection coefficient estimate used to discharge the open bound here.

The primary article by Alouges, Darses and Hillion (2022),
[Polynomial approximations in a generalized Nyman--Beurling criterion](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.5802/jtnb.1227/),
separates approximation and coefficient-control requirements in a related
generalized criterion. It is contextual, not a provider for our displacement.

The repository's QCRTCylinderProjection.legalCylinderApprox_error_sq
retains the unknown residual floor after legal projection.
QResidueConditional.conditional_leakage_floor prevents replacing that legal
projection by a contractive conditional expectation without proof.
The actual displacement envelope remains OPEN.
