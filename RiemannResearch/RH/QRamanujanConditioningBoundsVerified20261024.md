## Exact identities and the requested lower bound

Let P_n be the orthogonal projection onto the actual complete-Q stage generated by conductors 2 through n+1. For orthonormal auxiliary probes e_i, define

```text
A_ij = <P_n e_i, P_n e_j>_Q
B_ij = <e_i - P_n e_i, e_j - P_n e_j>_Q.
```

The new declarations prove the full matrix identity B = I-A, including every signed off-diagonal entry. They identify

```text
headLoss = trace(I-A)
         = sum_i ||e_i-P_n e_i||_Q^2
         = sum_i Prob(probe i is vacant).
```

The finite union bound gives `1-headLoss <= headMass = det A`, without assuming positive mass or independent projected probes. In particular, `headLoss <= 1-delta` implies `headMass >= delta`. A per-probe bound eta gives `headLoss <= card(heads)*eta`.

## Stronger determinant estimates

For a positive contraction A, with tau = trace(I-A), the formalization proves

```text
exp(-tau/epsilon) <= det A <= exp(-tau)
```

when every eigenvalue of A is at least epsilon > 0. The upper bound does not require the positive gap. A positive determinant lower bound delta forces both tau <= -log(delta) and every eigenvalue >= delta.

Thus, even for varying matrix sizes, eventual uniform positive determinant is equivalent to an eventual bounded trace defect together with a uniform positive lower eigenvalue bound. This is an exact reformulation of the mass condition, not its automatic arithmetic verification.

General declarations are in `LinearAlgebra/DeterminantTraceBounds.lean`. Actual Q specializations are `headMass_exp_lower_bound`, `headMass_exp_upper_bound`, and `headMass_trace_gap_iff`.

## A weaker sufficient conditioning hypothesis

Let U be the span of the auxiliary probes, and require the target qOne to be orthogonal to U. Assume, for every v in U,

```text
||v-P_n v||_Q^2 <= rho * ||v||_Q^2,    0 <= rho < 1.
```

This bound controls the whole span. Bounds only on individual probes are not substituted for it.

The formalization derives independence of the projected probes and proves

```text
(1-rho)*conditioningCost <= rho*packetError.
```

Together with Transfer 100's exact identity

```text
conditionedVacancy = packetError + conditioningCost,
```

this gives

```text
packetError <= conditionedVacancy <= packetError/(1-rho).
```

This comparison has no uniform determinant-mass hypothesis and no trace budget. Its geometric proof retains the exact cross-inner-product identity and uses Pythagoras and Cauchy-Schwarz.

General results are in `Analysis/ProjectionConditioningBound.lean`. Actual Q results are `conditioningCost_mul_le_of_loss_bound` and `conditionedVacancy_le_of_loss_bound` in `RH/QRamanujanDPPAngle.lean`.
