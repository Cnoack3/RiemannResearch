# Primary-source citation audit for the 5 August 2026 E8 memorandum

Artifact: serre_ii_e8_program_2026-08-05.pdf (51 pages).

Audited: 2026-08-07.

This file records what the cited sources actually prove. “Source checked”
does not mean Lean formalized: it means that the exact external statement,
hypotheses, and its use in the memorandum were inspected. Lean coverage is
recorded separately in SERRE_II_2026_08_05_THEOREM_LEDGER.md.

## Findings that change the theorem ledger

| Manuscript item | Citation verdict | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Theorem 2.1 | Exact support found for all three parts: Izquierdo--Lucchini Arteche Corollary 4.14, Garibaldi--Semenov (1.1)--(1.2), and Totaro Theorem 0.1. | Source checked; not formalized in Lean. |
| Theorem 3.2 | Evens--Jones supplies the tangent-weight calculation, and Brosnan supplies the motivic Bialynicki--Birula decomposition. Evens--Jones works over complex varieties and does not justify normalizing an arbitrary regular one-sided cocharacter merely by changing its sign. | The printed theorem is still false at that step. Choose a cocharacter in a specified chamber, or Weyl-conjugate and reindex; also supply the characteristic-zero descent argument. |
| Theorems 4.3 and 4.5 | Gille Corollaries 9.3.6 and 9.3.7 have exactly the stated hypotheses: 2, 3, and 5 invertible and scd_l(k) <= 2 for l = 2, 3, 5. | The Gille inputs are source confirmed. The earlier “exact scope unavailable” blocker is removed. |
| Theorem 4.6 and Proposition 4.7 | Sansuc Proposition 6.10 gives the required Picard--Brauer exact sequence for the homogeneous-space torsor, and Bayer-Fluckiger--Parimala covers the split nontrialitarian D8 classical group used here. Brauer purity supplies Proposition 4.7. | The arguments are source valid in characteristic zero, but the memorandum should pinpoint both cited results and add an explicit purity citation. |
| Theorem 4.9 | Chernousov--Gille--Merkurjev Theorem 8.2 is the required integral Rost-nilpotence theorem; Sechin--Semenov Lemma 7.8 is the required split-motive criterion. | The source-dependent motivic step is valid in characteristic zero. |
| Theorem 4.11 | The prime-to-p restriction/transfer proof is valid using oriented Rost nilpotence and Sechin--Semenov Lemma 7.8. Semenov Theorem 6.16 is narrower: its E8 assertion concerns p = 2 and heights at least 4, not every prime and positive height. | The theorem survives, but the final attribution to Semenov as the uniform proof must be replaced by the transfer argument plus Sechin--Semenov Section 4.10 and Lemma 7.8. |
| Theorem 5.4 | Gille 2001 Theorem 11 exactly gives cyclic degree 2, 3, and 5 injectivity under the standing characteristic-zero cohomological-dimension hypotheses. | Iteration over a solvable composition series is source valid. |
| Warning 5.5 | Gille LNM 2238 Theorem 8.4.1(2) is printed for every Galois extension of order 2^a 3^b 5^c, but its proof's induction to cyclic prime-degree layers does not cover nonsolvable A5 or S5. The 26 May 2026 errata repairs characteristic and degree-five inputs, not this group-theoretic reduction. | Keep the manuscript's warning. This issue does not invalidate Corollary 9.3.6: Proposition 9.3.5 supplies a solvable splitting extension, where Theorem 11 can be iterated. |
| Theorem 5.11 | The corrected Gille Lemma 1.6.5 says that a class directly R1-equivalent to 1 comes from some embedding mu_n -> T; the errata also explains that R1 H1 is generated by such direct classes. | The full-Weyl “no finite marker implies trivial R1 part” deduction uses the corrected statement correctly. |
| Theorem 5.13 | Harari--Szamuely Theorem 5.6 contains the exact Poitou--Tate segment used for a torus. Platonov--Rapinchuk Theorem 6.6 is the Hasse-principle input, while the nonarchimedean local vanishing should also be pinpointed (apparently Theorem 6.4). | The localization-surjectivity deduction is source valid; tighten the final global-neutrality citation. |
| Theorems 5.16 and 6.1 | Totaro's table on page 21 gives the 17 maximal-subgroup rows, subsystem stabilizers, indices, and the five O_8^+(2) conjugate survivor pairs. Gille Corollary 9.3.6 supplies the subsystem-source neutralization. | These citation inputs are confirmed; their algebraic descent arguments remain informal, not Lean proofs. |
| Theorem 5.6 setup | Totaro's table indirectly identifies the index-135 D8 stabilizer, but does not literally state the manuscript's sentence identifying every nonzero singular mod-2 vector with a toral involution having connected centralizer D8. | Add a lattice/centralizer proof or a direct reference for that sentence. |
| Conjecture 5.2 context | Geldhauser--Petrov Theorem 3.4 is an index-one-to-point theorem for selected E6 flags of type {1,2,6}; it is not literally a single-extension odd-degree theorem. | Say “an index-one-to-point (Springer-type) theorem is known for selected E6 flags.” |
| Theorem 7.2 | Izquierdo--Lucchini Arteche Corollary 4.14 gives index one; over a p-special field all closed-point degrees are powers of p, so a degree-one point follows. | Source valid. |
| Corollary 7.3 | Merkurjev Theorem 4 constructs a particular 2-special cohomological-dimension-two endpoint preserving the specially constructed tensor product of quaternion algebras and its associated anisotropic form. | The E8 endpoint exclusion is valid, but “preserving a chosen division algebra and anisotropic quadratic form” is broader than the cited theorem and should be narrowed. |

Two bibliographic issues remain: references [1] and [29] are invoked without
an exact theorem/proposition number, and reference [14] is never cited in the
text (it appears to be intended background for the HSpin16/EVIII setup).
