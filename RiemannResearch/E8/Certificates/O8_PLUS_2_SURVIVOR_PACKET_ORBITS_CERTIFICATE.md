# Survivor lattice and packet-orbit certificate

## Scope

This certificate extends O8_PLUS_2_COVER_MARKER_CERTIFICATE.md at the
triality-aligned configuration 2. It performs three finite calculations for
the 31 nonsplit, mod-2-fixed-free perfect-core rows:

1. recover integral 8 by 8 E8-lattice generators;
2. recover reduced words in the eight simple reflections;
3. restrict the corrected subsystem packet D8, (A2)^4, (A4)^2.

These are finite inputs to the proposed transporter calculation. They do not
prove the field-valued simultaneous common-Borel correction.

## Integral generators

For every TomLib SLP generator g, the integral matrix is recovered by

~~~gap
PreImagesRepresentative(actionHom, g);
~~~

GAP's WeylGroup(rootSystem) uses row vectors acting on the right in the
fundamental-weight basis. If C := CartanMatrix(rootSystem), the simple-root
basis matrix is C * M * C^-1.

The export has 31 rows and 64 generator matrices. Independent exact-integer
auditing verified, for every generator:

- determinant +1;
- preservation of the E8 Gram form;
- the declared generator order;
- preservation of the E8/2 quadratic form on all 256 vectors;
- the exact projective and 240-root lift action orders;
- membership of central -I in the generated lift.

All 31 common mod-2 fixed spaces have dimension zero.

Reduced simple-reflection words are recovered without a large-group
factorization:

~~~gap
slaWeylGroup := WeylGroupAsPermGroup(rootSystem);
slaSimplePerms := List([1 .. 8], i ->
    WeylWordAsPerm(rootSystem, [i]));
simpleToSla := GroupHomomorphismByImages(
    weylGroup, slaWeylGroup, simplePerms, slaSimplePerms);

ReflectionWordForLift := function(g)
    local p, word, reconstructed;
    p := Image(simpleToSla, g);
    word := PermAsWeylWord(rootSystem, p);
    reconstructed := Product(Reversed(word), i -> simplePerms[i]);
    Assert(0, reconstructed = g);
    return word;
end;
~~~

The reversal is required by SLA's documented anti-isomorphism convention.

Because each local subgroup is taken with its full inverse image in the
nonsplit central lift, generation of P implies generation of the full lift.

~~~gap
singularVectors := First(nonzeroVectorOrbits, o -> Length(o) = 135);
for class in alignedClasses do
    liftGenerators := EvaluateTomGenerators(class, standardLiftGenerators);
    P := Group(List(liftGenerators, g ->
        MatrixModTwo(PreImagesRepresentative(actionHom, g))));
    orbits := Orbits(P, singularVectors, OnRight);
    stabilizers := List(orbits, o -> Stabilizer(P, o[1], OnRight));
    closures := List(stabilizers, H -> NormalClosure(P, H));
    generated := Group(Concatenation(List(closures, GeneratorsOfGroup)));
    Assert(0, generated = P);
od;
~~~

### Finite E8.1 root-subsystem recovery

The root ordering in this subsection is GAP's one-based ordering

~~~gap
roots := Orbit(weylMatrixGroup, cartanMatrix[1], OnRight);
~~~

where roots are row vectors in the fundamental-weight basis and the Weyl
group acts on the right. For each packet class, the displayed simple-root
indices select a simple system for the recovered root subsystem. The two root
orbit lengths are respectively the subsystem roots and their complement in
the 240 E8 roots.

| subsystem | TomLib class | root orbits | simple-root indices | stabilizer in W(E8)^+ | subsystem even Weyl group | orientation quotient |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| D8 | 11166 | 112, 128 | 33, 40, 74, 118, 134, 163, 206, 239 | 2,580,480 | 2,580,480 | trivial |
| (A2)^4 | 11077 | 24, 216 | 6, 39, 109, 111, 134, 181, 211, 239 | 31,104 | 648 | GL(2,3), order 48 |
| (A4)^2 | 11076 | 40, 200 | 33, 74, 116, 149, 167, 170, 181, 217 | 28,800 | 7,200 | C4, order 4 |

GAP asserted in each row that the setwise stabilizer of the recovered root
subsystem inside W(E8)^+ is exactly the recovered full inverse-image lift of
the indicated TomLib subgroup. Thus the quotient orders in the final column
are exact stabilizer quotients, not merely abstract groups of the same order.

For every survivor and packet subgroup S, the orbits of P on Q/S were
computed. An orbit length is [P : P intersect g S g^-1]. For every row:

~~~text
sum(D8 orbit lengths)   = 135
sum(A2^4 orbit lengths) = 11200
sum(A4^2 orbit lengths) = 12096
gcd(all orbit lengths)  = 1
~~~

The final gcd assertion also follows without row-by-row arithmetic: a common
divisor of all restricted orbit lengths divides each of the three sums, and
gcd(135,11200,12096)=1.

~~~gap
packetClasses := [11166, 11077, 11076];
packetDegrees := [135, 11200, 12096];
packetSubgroups := List(packetClasses, c -> Group(
    EvaluateTomGenerators(c, standardQuotientGenerators)));
packetCosets := List(packetSubgroups, S -> RightCosets(orthogonalGroup, S));
packetActions := List(packetCosets, X ->
    ActionHomomorphism(orthogonalGroup, X, OnRight));

for class in alignedClasses do
    gens := EvaluateTomGenerators(class, standardQuotientGenerators);
    sizes := List([1 .. 3], j -> SortedList(List(Orbits(
        Group(List(gens, g -> Image(packetActions[j], g))),
        [1 .. packetDegrees[j]]), Length)));
    Assert(0, List(sizes, Sum) = packetDegrees);
    Assert(0, Gcd(Concatenation(sizes)) = 1);
od;
~~~

Thus the packet supplies the gcd-one finite-index family needed by additive
restriction--corestriction after a simultaneous reduction to one stable
Borel. The D8 calculation independently supplies subgroup generation.

## Remaining boundary

The matrices and words make the Weyl/Tits layer reproducible. They can
certify candidate Bruhat cells and the finite T[2] part of a normalizer
defect. They do not determine its field-valued torus factor. Consequently a
Weyl-cell contraction is not yet an E-rational correction.

The remaining theorem must construct simultaneous field-valued corrections
into one Galois-stable Borel, for every admissible toral-cocycle chart. Once
that is supplied, CoprimeRestrictionDetection and CoprimeTorusFiltration
remove the torus obstruction, and the unipotent filtration finishes.
